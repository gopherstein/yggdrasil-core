# Fixtures

Files the quality cases attach that are kept in the repository rather than
drawn by the test. Nothing here shows a person or a brand. The clip is ours,
made by the script beside it; the photos are public domain or CC0, from
Wikimedia Commons, shrunk to 640 pixels on the longer side with their
camera and location data removed (#449).
