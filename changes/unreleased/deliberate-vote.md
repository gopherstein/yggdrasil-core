### Added

- Deliberate, first part: a profile can set Deliberate to Always so its
  answers are drafted three times, independently, on other computers when
  there are any, and compared by their short final answers. When most
  drafts agree, that answer is kept; the answer's steps say whether they
  agreed, and the drafts are kept with the answer for the app to show.
  It's off by default while it's measured, and a judge for drafts that
  disagree comes next.
