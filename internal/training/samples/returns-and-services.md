# Tread Right Tires: policies and services

## Returns

Unmounted tires can be returned within 30 days with the receipt. Mounted tires can be returned within 30 days only if they have fewer than 500 miles and no damage. Special-order tires are final sale.

## Installation

Installation is $25 per tire and includes mounting, balancing, new valve stems, and disposal of old tires. TPMS sensor service is $10 per sensor.

## Road hazard protection

Road hazard protection costs 12% of the tire price. It covers repair or replacement for punctures, cuts, and impact damage for 3 years or the first 2/32 inch of wear, whichever comes first.

## Rotation and alignment

Free tire rotation every 5,000 miles for tires bought here. A four-wheel alignment check is free with a set of four tires; a full alignment is $99.

## Hours

Monday to Friday 8 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 8 AM to 4 PM, closed Sunday. Walk-ins are welcome; appointments are served first.
